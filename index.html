ℹ️ Dashboard Summary & Architecture

Dynamic Time-Flexing (Fluid Time): Clinical capacity is governed by Total Clinical Hours (30 min per PS, 60 min per Eval). When the stateless global PFA pool absorbs a Phone Screen, 30 minutes of clinical time is dynamically refunded to licensed CEs to reinvest into Clinical Evaluation capacity.

Per-Month Funnel Conversion Stamps: Adjusting any conversion rate in the Unified Arbitrage Funnel stamps those rates to the active month (📌 MONTH STAMP) without distorting other months, and feeds downstream M-1 / M-2 / M-3 carryover cohorts.

Capacity Potential & Marketing Headroom: The right-hand column displays your roster's true physical slot ceiling across stages, translating the tighter stage bottleneck into exact additional Accepted Apps Marketing can send.

Cohort Actuals Calibrator: Lock historical enrollment actuals per month (🔒 ACTUALS) and sync rolling-average timing shape—or full empirical yield + shape—for forward projections.
