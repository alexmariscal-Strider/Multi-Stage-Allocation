ℹ️ Dashboard Summary & Architecture

What it does: End-to-end tactical alignment (Demand Pipeline vs. Maximum Capacity Potential).

Core Focus: Definitive Command Center tracking where pipeline demand collides with strict clinical capacity.

In-Month vs. Row-Based Lagged Cohort Logic

Analyzing everything strictly "In-Month" creates artificial capacity panics because clinical labor and enrollments occur days or weeks after an application arrives. The Lagged Cohort Engine tracks each monthly intake cohort horizontally across its 30+ day journey:

  • 📞 Phone Screens (100% In-Month): Because median turnaround is ~3–4 days, ~100% of PS labor hits the current month.
  • 📋 Evaluations (Split Over 2 Months): Due to an ~11–14 day median time-to-eval, evaluation workload straddles two months (configurable 50% current month intake + 50% carried over from prior month's intake).
  • 🎓 Enrollments (4-Month Row-Based Lag Curve): Rather than relying on volatile column-based ratios, enrollments follow an empirical cohort realization curve tied directly to each historical month's accepted application cohort (default: 5.0% Month 0, 22.0% Month +1, 5.5% Month +2, and 1.3% Month +3 = 33.8% cumulative conversion), dynamically scaled by your live funnel sliders and capped by physical capacity limits.

Need-Based PFA Arbitrage Routing (State Heatmap)

Rather than wasting PFA capacity on states where assigned CEs already have surplus screen capacity, the engine pools 100% of active PFA capacity and deploys it exclusively where needed:

  • 🚨 PFA: PS OVERFLOW (Tier 1 Rescue): Triggered when Gross PS Demand exceeds CE PS Supply. PFAs absorb the shortfall first so prospective patients do not drop off at the front door.
  • ⭐ PFA: SHIELD EVALS (Tier 2 Reinvestment): Remaining PFA capacity is swept into states where Evaluations are below 90% coverage. Offloading a Phone Screen frees up 30 minutes of clinical time that CEs reinvest into Evaluations.
  • ✅ CE SELF-SUFFICIENT (0 PS PFA Target): Assigned CEs cover 100% of Phone Screens on their own with zero PFA assistance required.
